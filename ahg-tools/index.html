Business-day holidays

The purchase and sale agreement (Idaho REALTORS RE-21, section 26) counts business days as Monday to Friday, leaving out the legal holidays in Idaho Code 73-108. A holiday on a Saturday is observed the Friday before, one on a Sunday the Monday after. Juneteenth is a federal holiday but not an Idaho one, so it isn't here. The pending tracker's deadlines skip these days.

Year
Adds that year's Idaho holidays by the 73-108 rules. Days added by hand stay.

73-108 also counts any day the President or the Governor appoints, so add those by hand when one is declared.
