Privacy

ShawnZapps is a handful of small hobby apps made by Shawn Anderson in Idaho Falls, Idaho, for family and friends. This page covers the apps that ask you to sign in, such as Dough. Last updated October 4, 2026.

What is collected

  • Your sign-in. Your email address, and a password you choose or, if you use Continue with Google, your name and email from Google. Nothing else is requested from your Google account.
  • What you put in. In Dough, that is your recipes, your bakes, your notes and ratings, and the display name you choose for sharing.
  • Basic technical records. The hosting services keep ordinary logs, such as sign-in times and errors, to keep things running.

How it is used

Only to run the apps for you: to sign you in, save your things, and show them back to you. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone for their own purposes.

Who can see your things

Only you, unless you choose to share. If you create a share link for a recipe, anyone who has that link can see the recipe, your notes on it, your display name, and your rated bakes of it, until you stop sharing. Shawn can see stored data as the person who runs the apps and looks at it only to fix problems.

Services that handle the data

  • Supabase stores accounts and app data, on servers in the United States.
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Anthropic, only when you use an AI feature. The recipe and bake details needed to answer are sent to Anthropic's Claude to produce the reply.
  • GitHub Pages serves the web pages.

Keeping and deleting

Your data stays until you delete it or ask for your account to be removed. You can delete recipes and bakes yourself in the app. To delete your whole account, ask Shawn, using the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen, and it will be removed along with everything in it.

Children

These apps are not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date at the top will change and people using the apps will be told.
