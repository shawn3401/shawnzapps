Your Turn

Where should we eat?

Your Turn settles it the way you already do, just faster.

  1. Tap who's eating and answer a few quick questions: how much time, how far, what sounds good.
  2. It deals a handful of places that fit, and skips the ones you've been to lately.
  3. Everyone gets to rule one out. Whoever's turn it is makes the final call.

It needs an account so it can remember your places, your ratings, and whose turn it is.
