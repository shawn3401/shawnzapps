### How the calculator works
- Modeled on Dough Guy's calculator (doughguy.co/pages/dough), which Shawn was using. His fixed recipe is the starting preset (`PRESET`): 62% water, 0.4% active dry yeast, 2.5% salt, 2% sugar, 3.3% olive oil, all as a percent of flour.
- Thickness is grams of dough per square inch. `THICK` has Dough Guy's thin, regular, thick (2.04, 2.39, 3.11; that is 409, 480, 626 g for a 16 inch pizza) plus two halfway stops added Oct 7, 2026. Ball weight = thickness x area; flour = total dough / (1 + sum of percents).
- Unlike his, every ratio has a slider, thickness is a slider, and there are two more inputs: how long until baking (`FERMENT`: Quick about 2 hours, Same day 4 to 6 hours, Overnight 1 day in the fridge, 2 to 4 days; renamed Oct 3, 2026 after Shawn found "Tonight" and "Today" confusing, since tonight was the faster one) and yeast type (`YEAST`: active dry, instant x0.75, fresh x2.5). Picking a ferment plan sets a starting yeast percent and changes the steps. The yeast numbers for the shorter plans are Claude's rule-of-thumb starting points, not tested by Shawn; adjust them from his reports.
- Active dry yeast in cold water (Shawn's question, Oct 3, 2026): Dough Guy's steps stir it straight into cold water, but the yeast package says warm. The cold plans now bloom it in about 60 g of the water warmed to 105°F and keep the rest cold. Same-day plans use all warm water.
- "Lock it in" inserts a row in `dough_bakes` (the plan snapshot). "How did it go?" adds a 1 to 5 rating, went well, went poorly, and "next time, try". The newest bake and the newest "next time" note show in a banner above the calculator. "Make it again" loads a bake back into the calculator.
- Flour is a dropdown (Shawn, Oct 3, 2026: no retyping). `renderFlours()` builds it from the distinct `flour_name` values in that person's bakes, newest first, plus "Add a new flour…" which reveals the text box. With no bakes yet, only the text box shows. There is no separate flours table.
- Calculator state and the flour name are remembered in localStorage as a convenience only.

### Making the next batch better (Oct 7, 2026)
Shawn asked for the ideal flow for sitting down to make another batch of an existing recipe with tweaks from the last bake, approved it, and added two requests of his own. What was built:
- Thickness is five fixed stops, not a slider (Shawn: the slider was too subjective): Thin, Thin+, Regular, Regular+, Thick (`THICK`; the + stops are halfway to the next). `buildControls()` snaps any older value to the nearest stop, and `thickText()` names the nearest stop.
- Grams are nested under the ratios (Shawn was irritated by the distance between the two cards). One "Ingredients" card: per ingredient a tick box, name, a gram box you can type over, the percent, and its slider directly underneath (`ingRow`, updated in place by `setRow` in `render()` so sliders and focused boxes are not rebuilt). The separate "Weigh this out" card is gone. Left column: The plan, Ingredients. Right column: Steps, Recipe notes and links, the author's bakes, Lock it in.
- "Last time" card above the calculator (a `<details>`, open by default): the newest bake of the recipe on screen with its stars, what changed that time, notes, went well, went poorly, next time, along-the-way entries, photos, and buttons for the report and "Use that bake's numbers".
- "Suggest changes for this batch" on that card (AI access only, bake must be rated): Edge Function `dough-tweak` (`supabase/dough/functions/dough-tweak/index.ts`, `suggest_changes` tool, no web search, same access list and cap) returns a summary, zero to two ratio changes each with a reason, and up to three non-ratio tips. Each ratio change has its own Apply button. Saved on the bake (`dough_bakes.suggest`) so it is paid for once; "Ask again" refetches.
- "Different from last time" box at the bottom of the Ingredients card (`diffFrom`): ratios (planned versus planned), yeast type, timing, thickness, flour. Saved with the bake as `dough_bakes.changes` (lines of text) and shown on the bake card and the next Last time card.
- Lock it in NO LONGER writes ratios to the recipe (it still saves steps and batch size). A change has to earn its place: the report dialog shows "This bake used different ratios than the recipe" with Keep them or Don't keep them (`keepPlan`, `dough_bakes.kept`), preselected Keep at 4 or 5 stars and Don't keep at 3 or fewer, and the recipe's ingredients change only on Keep. A yeast amount borrowed for a different timing is never written to the recipe. Until a bake is rated, the recipe still shows its old ratios.
- `supabase/dough/009_bake_changes.sql` adds `changes`, `suggest`, `kept`.

### Sharing (Oct 4, 2026)
- Shawn's brother-in-law wanted the cinnamon roll recipe. Decisions Shawn made: anyone with the link can open it, no account; the link always shows the author's CURRENT recipe; it carries the ingredients, steps, recipe notes, the author's name, their ratings and reports (reported bakes only, with their along-the-way updates), and saved timing suggestions; and the RECEIVER chooses what to do with it.
- Author side: "Share" in the recipe row opens a dialog that asks for a display name (`dough_profiles`, asked once) and creates `dough_types.share_token`. Link is `/dough/?r=<token>`. "Stop sharing" clears the token. The button reads "Shared ✓" while a link exists.
- `dough_shared(token)` (`supabase/dough/007_sharing.sql`) is a security definer function open to anon. It returns `{author, own_id, recipe, bakes}` for a live token and null otherwise. It is the only way anyone reads another person's recipe; table RLS is unchanged.
- Receiver side: the link opens the recipe in the calculator as a preview (`s.sharedTok`), with a bar at the top. Signed out: "Sign in to keep this recipe". Signed in, two choices, worded for Shawn's request:
  - "Follow <author>'s recipe": their own `dough_types` row with `follows_token` and `author_name`. Read-only mirror: `refreshFollowed()` re-reads the author's version on every load. They can size it, borrow a timing suggestion, and log bakes, but steps, notes, and Edit are locked, and lock-in only saves their batch size. The row's button is "Following <author>", which offers "Make it my own copy" or "Remove it".
  - "Make my own copy": an ordinary row with `copied_from` (shown under the recipe notes). No link back.
- If the author stops sharing or deletes the recipe, a followed row turns into an ordinary copy on the follower's next load, with a one-time notice. A followed recipe cannot be re-shared. An author opening their own link, or someone already following it, is taken straight to their recipe.
- The author's reported bakes show in an "<author>'s bakes" card under the recipe notes, for the link preview and for followers.
- Not built: sharing with named people only, a list of who follows a recipe, sharing a whole category. The AI helper stays allow-list only, so a new person who follows a recipe sees saved suggestions but cannot request new ones.

### Dough types and steps (Oct 3, 2026)
- `supabase/dough/002_types_and_steps.sql`. Table `dough_types`, one row per person per type: `name`, `sizing`, `ingredients` (`[{key, name, pct}]`, flour is always 100 and not listed), `steps` (text, one per line, null = none written), `defaults` (the plan from the last locked-in bake). Everyone gets a "Pizza" type on first sign-in (`loadTypes()` inserts it and attaches older bakes to it). Signed out, the page uses an in-memory Pizza type only.
- `sizing` is how a batch is sized: `pizza` (count, diameter, thickness), `pieces` (count and grams each, for loaves and rolls), `flour` (start from the flour weight). The type bar above the calculator switches types; "New type" and "Edit <name>" open the type dialog (name, sizing, ingredient rows). New types start with Water, Yeast, Salt rows and EMPTY percents on purpose: never prefill a recipe Shawn did not give.
- Ingredient rows in the type dialog have up and down arrows (Shawn, Oct 3, 2026, wanted water at the top). The saved order is the order of the sliders and the weigh list.
- An ingredient whose name contains "yeast" gets key `yeast`, which turns on the yeast type switch. Water, salt, sugar, and oil keys get tuned slider ranges (`RANGES`); other ingredients get a range from their own percent.
- Steps belong to the person, not the app (Shawn, Oct 3, 2026: "not sure we want to get in the business of giving people steps"; his own are a mix of Dough Guy and Leo Spizzirri). The Steps card has "Edit steps" (a textarea, one per line). The generated basic steps only show for a pizza-sized type whose steps have never been edited, labeled as a starting point.
- Lock it in saves a snapshot on the bake (`type_id`, `type_name`, `sizing`, `ingredients`, `steps`) and writes the steps and plan back to the type. Since Oct 7, 2026 it does not write the ratios; see "Making the next batch better". The old `pct` column is only on bakes from before this change. The yeast starting-point hint and the auto yeast change on ferment plan apply to pizza sizing only.
- Plan versus actual (Shawn, Oct 3, 2026: nobody should chase sliders to hit a gram): every number in the weigh list is a field. Typing the flour (`s.flour_fix`) re-scales the whole batch to that flour. Typing any other ingredient (`s.actual[key]`) records what went in and shows its true percent. Changing the plan sliders clears typed numbers (`replan()`). The bake saves the true percent in `pct` with the planned one in `plan_pct`; the type keeps the planned ratios, so an overpour does not become the recipe.
- Timing (settled Oct 4, 2026 after two false starts). Shawn's rule: ONE master recipe, never copies that differ only by timing, because a salt change would have to be made in each. A recipe is written for one timing (`dough_types.timing`, set in the Edit dialog as "This recipe is written for"; `s.base` in the page). The four "How long until you bake?" cards stay; the recipe's own card has a gold underline and opens selected. Tapping a different card changes NOTHING in the recipe or steps. A box under the steps, "Suggestions based on your timing", shows two to four AI suggestions for speeding up or slowing down, a suggested yeast percent with a "Use it" link (for this bake only; it reverts when he taps back to the recipe's timing, and lock-in at another timing does not write that yeast to the recipe), a one-line tradeoff note, and "Make <timing> this recipe's timing". Suggestions come from Edge Function `dough-adapt` (`timing_tips` tool, no web search), are fetched automatically on tap for people with AI access, and are cached in `dough_types.tips` keyed by timing with a signature of the steps and ingredient names so they are paid for once. Without AI access the box shows a generic rule of thumb. Rejected along the way: per-timing step versions inside a recipe (`variants`, no longer read), and Claude's idea of dropping the cards and making each timing its own recipe. Still not built: a clock schedule worked backward from when he wants to eat.
- Recipe notes (Shawn, Oct 4, 2026): a "Recipe notes" card under Steps for notes about the recipe itself, not a bake (bakes have their own notes and report). `dough_types.notes`, saved straight to the recipe when the box loses focus, shown only for a saved recipe when signed in, and passed to the AI as context. Not copied onto bakes.
- Updates along the way (Shawn, Oct 4, 2026: rising happens over hours and days, and he wanted to capture "8 am, rolls out of the fridge for the final rise" before the final rating). Each bake card in My bakes has an "Along the way" list with a text box, a time (defaults to now, editable for logging after the fact), and Add. Stored in `dough_bakes.log` as `[{at, text}]`, oldest first (`supabase/dough/006_bake_log.sql`). Entries can be removed. The AI sees them with the bake's report.
- Photos on a bake (Shawn, Oct 4, 2026, after the cinnamon rolls were "a huge success"): each bake card has "Add photos" (up to 12). The browser shrinks each to a 1600px JPEG (`shrink()`), uploads to the public-read Storage bucket `dough-photos` at `<user id>/<bake id>/<uuid>.jpg`, and stores the paths in `dough_bakes.photos`. Tapping a thumbnail opens it large with "Remove photo". Storage policies let a signed-in person add, list, and delete only under their own folder; the bucket is public-read at unguessable paths so shared recipes can show the author's photos (`supabase/dough/008_photos_and_links.sql`). Deleting a bake leaves its photo files behind, which is harmless. No faces rule here; these are his own food photos.
- Links on a recipe (same day; he wanted the PlanToEat icing recipe tied to the cinnamon rolls): the "Recipe notes and links" card has a list of links, each a name and a web address, saved to `dough_types.links` as `[{label, url}]`. They belong to the recipe, not a bake, and travel with a shared recipe. Only http and https addresses are accepted.
- My bakes shows type filter pills once there are two or more types. The "Last time" banner follows the type on screen.

### Ask AI (Oct 3, 2026)
- Shawn's idea: chat with AI to fill in a dough type, telling it who to look to for inspiration (ChainBaker, Leo Spizzirri), then edit before saving. Later it should read his reports and suggest the next change.
- Edge Function `dough-ai` in the ShawnZapps project; source in `supabase/dough/functions/dough-ai/index.ts` (deploy with the Supabase tools, `verify_jwt` on). It calls Claude (`claude-opus-5-5`, effort low, web search with `max_uses` 4) through the Anthropic SDK. Secret `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` was set by Shawn in Edge Functions, Secrets (its own key, created for this project). Never put it in the repo or the page.
- The model replies in plain text and, when it has a formula, calls the `propose_dough` tool (strict schema: summary, name, sizing, count, piece_g, flour_g, yeast_type, ingredients, steps, sources). The function returns `{reply, proposal, remaining}`. The page sends the chat as text plus `aiContext()` (current type, plan, ratios, steps, the last four bakes of that type with their reports).
- On the page: "Ask AI" pill in the type bar and a link in the New type dialog, both only when the person has access. The proposal card offers "Save as a new type" (inserts a `dough_types` row) or "Use in <current type>" (loads ratios and steps into the calculator, not saved until Lock it in). The chat lives in memory only and is gone on reload.
- Access and cost control, `supabase/dough/003_ai.sql`: `dough_ai_access` (one row per allowed user, `daily_limit` default 40 calls per 24 hours) and `dough_ai_usage` (one row per call with token counts). Signups are open, so the helper is allow-list only; Shawn's account is the only one on it. To add a friend: `insert into dough_ai_access (user_id) select id from auth.users where email = '...'`.
- The prompt tells it to write steps in its own words, name its sources, and say what is the source's number versus its own judgment.

## yourturn/ app (started Oct 4, 2026)

### What it is
shawnzapps.com/yourturn/. Shawn and Leah's way to settle where to eat, also for double dates and business lunches. Named for "it's your turn to pick". It copies their real ritual: one person throws out options, the others rule some out, and whoever's turn it is decides. It may become a mobile app later, so it is phone-first (one 680px column) and everything lives behind Supabase, nothing in the page but display logic.

### Files
- Two Google accounts (Shawn, Oct 14, 2026): on his iPhone, Google sign-in quietly used his work account (shawn@andersonhicks.com, a separate ShawnZapps account made Oct 13) instead of shawn3401@gmail.com, so Your Turn opened empty and asked for his name. The work account got onto Your Turn through another app: every app on shawnzapps.com uses the same Supabase client with the default storage key, so a sign-in in one app (Listing Assistant, Dough) signs you into all of them in that browser. Your Turn itself had no Google button until Oct 14, 2026 (`GOOGLE_ENABLED` was a hard false); it now reads `/auth/v1/settings` at load like Dough. Now Google sign-in always shows Google's account chooser (`prompt: "select_account"`), and the first-sign-in "What should people call you?" dialog says which email is signed in, with "Sign out and use another account". Dough and Listing Assistant have the same Google button without the chooser.
- `yourturn/index.html` the whole app, CSS and JS inline, supabase-js v2 from jsDelivr. Same sign-in code as Dough; the Google button shows when Supabase Auth's public settings say Google is on. Teal and mustard palette, Fraunces and Source Sans 3.
- `supabase/yourturn/001_schema.sql` tables, RLS, and functions. Safe to re-run.
- `supabase/yourturn/functions/yourturn-search/index.ts` the Edge Function that talks to Google Places (deploy with the Supabase tools, `verify_jwt` on).
- Linked from the home page under Personal, along with Dough.

### Decisions (Shawn, Oct 4, 2026)
- Separate accounts with sharing, and separate ratings per person. Up to 6 at the table (`MAX_PARTY`), some with accounts and some guests. People are remembered in a list so adding them is one tap.
- A round is played on ONE phone passed around. Joining from your own phone is a possible later step.
- Two ways to play, chosen at the top of the Pick tab (Shawn, Oct 4, 2026; this replaced the first version's "everyone gets one rule-out"). See "Two ways to play" below.
- Works anywhere, but NEVER bulk-loads an area. Searches are live and nothing is saved until someone touches a place (rates it, visits it, marks it want-to-try, or corrects a fact). That is why there is no separate travel mode.
- Facts about a place are shared by everyone; opinions and history are personal.

### Data (ShawnZapps project, tables prefixed `yourturn_`)
- `yourturn_profiles` display name and a saved home spot (set from the phone's location on the People tab). Own row only.
- `yourturn_people` each person's "people I eat with" list. A row is a guest (name only), an invite (`invite_email` set), or linked (`linked_user_id` set). The client cannot write `linked_user_id` (column grants); only `yourturn_accept()` sets it, so linking takes both sides. Invites send NO email: the other person sees the invite on their People tab when they sign in with that email (`yourturn_invites()`), and accepting adds each to the other's list. Deleting a linked row unlinks the other side (trigger).
- `yourturn_places` one shared row per restaurant anyone has touched, keyed by Google place id. Shared facts anyone signed in can correct: `genre`, `style` (fast_food, fast_casual, sit_down; `fine` is retired and read as sit_down), `minutes` (typical time in and out), `waits` (rarely, peak, usually). `name`, `address`, `lat`, `lng` are a cache of Google's listing with `refreshed_at`. Google's rating, review count, price, and hours are never stored; they come live with each search.
- `yourturn_ratings` per person per place: `rating` 1 to 5, `never_again`, `want_to_try`, `rest_days` (days before suggesting it again, null = `DEFAULT_REST` 14), `notes`. Readable by the owner and by linked people.
- `yourturn_visits` where they went: `visited_on`, `party` (`[{key, name}]`, key is a user id or `g:name` for a guest), `user_ids` (the accounts present), `group_key` (sorted party keys), `picker_key`, `picker_name`. Visible to anyone in `user_ids` and to people linked with them. You can only put yourself and linked accounts on a visit (`yourturn_party_ok`).
- `yourturn_usage` one row per search for the caps. Service role only.

### Two ways to play (Shawn, Oct 4, 2026)
- "Who's eating?" is the first question and "How are you deciding?" the second: Take turns or Elimination (`R.mode`). Shawn swapped them on Oct 5, 2026 (who first, then the format); the mode question and the group panel under it only appear once a second person is tapped in. Each group remembers the way it last played (`modes` in localStorage, falling back to its newest visit's `mode`), and tapping people in or out switches to that group's way (`recallMode`). It can always be changed. With one person there is no mode.
- A group is the exact set of people at the table (`group_key`). Shawn and Leah are one group; Shawn, Leah, Natalie, and Evan are another with its own rotation. Under "How are you deciding?" a panel shows whose turn it is for that group and its last five meals with who picked, or "First time out with this group."
- First time out for a group (in either mode), the phone's owner goes LAST among people who have never picked (Shawn, Oct 5, 2026: the person who suggests the app should not get the first pick). On a brand-new group two phones can therefore disagree until the first meal is logged; after that history decides.
- Because of that, history lines (the group panel and the History tab) say "you picked in Take turns mode" or "in Elimination mode" (Shawn, Oct 5, 2026, after "I picked last but my pick now?").
- Rotations are SEPARATE per mode (Shawn's call): `yourturn_visits.mode` is 'turns' or 'elim' (`003_visit_mode.sql`), and `turnOrder(pl, mode)` only counts that mode's visits. Visits with no mode count as turns.

- Elimination: deal twice the number of diners. ### How a round works (Pick tab)
- Questions: who's eating, time available (door to door), how far to drive (5, 10, 15, 25 min one way), kind of place (any mix of the four styles, none = anything), what sounds good (tap once = want, twice = not that), willing to wait, starting from (where I am, Home, or a town looked up with Open-Meteo's free geocoder). Answers are remembered in localStorage.
- "Find us a place" calls `yourturn-search`, which makes two Nearby Search calls (by popularity and by distance, 20 results each, merged). Places the table rated 4+ or wants to try are added from saved data if they are in range, tagged "Hours not checked".
- `judge()` drops a place, with a reason shown under "left out, and why", when: anyone present said never again, the genre was ruled out, wrong style, closed, closing too soon, too long for the time (2 x drive + minutes), a wait they do not want (`usually`, or `peak` during 11:45 to 1 or 5:30 to 8), visited within its rest days by anyone present, or the same genre eaten in the last `GENRE_REST` (2) days. Otherwise score = the table's average rating (Google's minus 0.5 if nobody has rated it), plus a bump for want-to-try and wanted genres, plus some randomness. Top `DEAL` (5) are dealt; "Deal 5 more" shows the next ones without another Google call.
- Starting minutes in and out (`STYLES`): fast food 20, fast casual 30, sit-down 50. Lowered from 35 and 70 on Oct 14, 2026 (Shawn): at 70, "1 hour" ruled out every sit-down place even next door. Only a time someone sets in Details is saved to `yourturn_places.minutes`; logging a meal no longer copies the guess in, and copied guesses on rows nobody edited were cleared to null so the new numbers apply.

"Go here" logs the visit (today, the party, the picker), which is what advances the turn. "We changed our minds" deletes it.

- `ahg-tools/import/access_import.py` (run `python3 -I ahg-tools/import/access_import.py` from the repo root) copies every backend table into `public` in one transaction: creates missing tables, empties all copied tables, reloads from the .accdb, resets id counters, adds Access's enforced relationships as foreign keys (`not valid`).
